# Glossary

**Alignment check:** A test video that FacadeKit sends to FPP, showing the outline of your house. Use it to see whether the picture is shifted.

**Blend mode:** The way a clip's pixels mix with the picture behind them. **Add** and **Screen** make black areas show as no light, so they suit videos with a black background.

**FPP:** Falcon Player, free software that runs on a Raspberry Pi and plays your show on the projector.

**Keystone:** A projector setting that corrects a picture that is wider at the top or bottom. Turn it off for FacadeKit.

**Native resolution:** The resolution the projector's screen is built for, such as 1920×1080. It is listed in the projector's manual or specifications.

**Playlist:** The list of items FPP plays. Your rendered show is sent to FPP to be played from there.

**Projector view:** The fullscreen window FacadeKit opens on the projector. It shows the grid during setup and your show during preview.

**Render:** To build the video file for your show, at a chosen quality preset. Use **Render show** on the **Publish** page.

**Surface:** A flat area of the house, such as the garage door, where you place a video clip.
